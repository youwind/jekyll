I'm a student, can I use Youwind for my master thesis or PhD?
Yes, we do provide student licenses prior to a preliminary interview to get to know the content of your work and how we can support you best.
Can I use Youwind for different countries?
Yes, you will be able to use Youwind on a global scale.

We will be pleased to do so including the level of uncertainty you will be comfortable with; in case it is useful for our client.

Yes, contact us and we will evaluate the R&D proposal internally and get back to you.
