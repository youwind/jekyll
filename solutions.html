Frequently Asked Questions

All FAQs
What wake loss models does Youwind include?
Youwind includes the state-of-the-art wake loss models such as TurbOPark, N.O. Jensen and Ainslie Eddy Viscosity model. It is also possible to perform a weighted average of these wake loss models.
Does Youwind approach wake losses in a different ways when analysing offshore or onshore wind farms?
Yes, in onshore wind farms the orography is taken into account for the calculations.
Can Youwind include the impact of neighbouring wind farms in the energy yield?
Yes, the user can create any existing or expected wind farm and store it in its' own library item. Therefore, when calculating the wake losses you will be able to select the neighbouring wind farms to be considered for the external wake losses.
What GIS layers are included in the Youwind platform?
Youwind includes relevant areas for your wind farm design that are publically available, from designated areas for wind farms to technical and environmental constraints, human activities,such as vessel density, fishing activities, existing infrastructure of cables and pipelines and other useful data for calculations, such as harbours and onshore electrical substations. Youwind also displays the depth contour and the wind map from Vortex FdC.
