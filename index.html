Frequently Asked Questions

ALL FAQS
How does the onboarding process work?
The onboarding procedure consists of dedicated onboarding sessions for a reduced group of users where we go through a real case that is chosen by the client, to go over all functionalities of the IT solution and discuss Q&A while users are trying to do it by themselves.
Can Youwind be used for onshore and offshore wind farms?
Yes, Youwind supports both onshore and offshore wind farms. When you create a scenario of your wind farm you will be able to select if it is an onshore, offshore or a combination of both.
Which wind resource can be included in Youwind?
Youwind supports a broad range of wind data sources and formats. Youwind includes by default the Vortex Maps with ERA5, 20 years average with 1km resolution that has been mesoscaled. Optionally, the user will be able to upload wind resource grids and timeseries.
